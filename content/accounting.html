How profit is calculated

A record of this trip’s harvest and costs, rather than the lifetime profit of each patch.

Estimated profit = harvest value − supplies used

Harvest value

Each recorded item uses a GE price snapshot. Picking fruit from an already-planted dragonfruit tree counts the fruit without inventing an old sapling cost. Moving, banking, or noting produce does not count as a fresh harvest.

Herb-sack entries inferred from Farming XP are labeled. See Herb sacks for how those counts work.

Costs included automatically

  • Visible seed and sapling consumption during planting.
  • Supported successful compost treatments. A bottomless compost application uses half the price of one bucket.
  • Supported gardener payments with recent nearby patch context.
  • Consumed icy/stony basalt, supported teleport tablets, and loose inventory teleport scrolls during an active run.

Costs to add manually

Use Add / correct entry for spell runes, rune-pouch supplies, jewellery charges, master scroll-book teleports, Fertile Soil, seed-box consumption, or missed gardener payments. Start a run before traveling if you want its first teleport included.

Example

If your harvest is valued at 100,000 gp and this trip’s supplies cost 30,000 gp, estimated profit is 70,000 gp. These are illustrative values; your prices and quantities will differ.

What the total excludes: historical planting costs, GE sale tax, and the price you actually paid or sold for. Items without a GE price are valued at zero and flagged for review.

Farming scope

The harvest catalogue covers herbs, allotments, flowers, hops, bushes, cactus, fruit trees, seaweed, mushrooms, belladonna, grapes, calquat, celastrus, crystal trees, and coral. Detection coverage varies by crop and event sequence; check unexpected totals and report mismatches.

Woodcutting logs, combat drops, Hespori combat loot, farming contracts, birdhouses, and minigame rewards are outside this tracker’s automatic accounting.
