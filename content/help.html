What to include in a bug report

  • The plugin and RuneLite versions.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  • For farming counts: crop, patch, sack state, before/after quantities, outfit, and relevant diary bonuses.
  • A screenshot of the relevant ledger entries, if helpful.

Search existing issues first. If someone has reported the same problem, add your details to that report.

Issues are public. Remove account identifiers and private information from screenshots. Don’t post credentials or full client logs. Support is handled through the issue tracker; there is no guaranteed response time.
