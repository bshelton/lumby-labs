Lead the Way

Pick a destination and follow one waypoint at a time. The gold step in the sidebar tells you what to do next.

Installation. Open the Plugin Hub in RuneLite, search for Lead the Way by Lumby Labs, and select Install. View the Plugin Hub listing.

Start a guide

  1. Open the gold signpost sidebar and choose Hill Giants or Dagannoth Kings.
  2. Open your bank to check what to withdraw. Carried and equipped items count toward the checklist.
  3. Select Guide me. Follow the cyan minimap cue toward the waypoint. When the tile comes into view, look for its outline. The world map also shows a gold signpost.

Current destinations

The first two routes guide you to the Hill Giants in Edgeville Dungeon and the Dagannoth Kings entrance. More destinations are planned.

Hill Giants · Free-to-play · Medium. Start at Edgeville bank and enter the trapdoor south of the bank. The walking route avoids the Agility pipe and takes you past the brass key. If you already have the key near the Grand Exchange, the guide can send you through the locked shed instead.

Dagannoth Kings · Members · Very long. Check the suggested approach before leaving the bank. The northern solo route uses a pet rock at the pressure door and a rune thrownaxe at the barricade. Pack your own combat supplies.

Adjust the on-screen instruction

Use Settings in the sidebar header (the wrench on narrow screens) to toggle Show canvas helper. To move the banner, hold your RuneLite overlay drag key and drag it. The default is Option on Mac or Alt on Windows.

For a misplaced marker or an unexpected route, include your destination, where you were standing, and the instruction you saw. Get help on GitHub.
