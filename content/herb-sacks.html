Herb sacks

Herbs can be counted even when they go straight into an open sack without a chat message.

How the count works

The plugin combines visible inventory gains and supported sack messages with Farming XP inference. It identifies the herb patch you clicked, observes the XP gained while harvesting, and estimates herbs that weren’t already observed.

The inference accounts for Farmer’s outfit and Falador Diary XP bonuses and separates final-pick completion XP. Visible inventory gains are subtracted so herbs overflowing into your inventory aren’t also recorded as inferred sack herbs.

This is not a direct read of your sack. Entries inferred from XP are labeled Sack harvest — XP inference and produce a review note. Checking the sack is useful for verifying counts, but is not required to trigger tracking.

Verify a harvest

  1. Check the sack and inventory quantities before harvesting.
  2. Click the herb patch and harvest normally.
  3. Check the quantities afterward. Include any herbs that went into the inventory.
  4. Compare that increase with the ledger’s harvest quantity.

When inference may not work

An unknown patch state, unexpected XP, or the 200 million Farming XP cap can prevent a reliable estimate. Inference is disabled on seasonal, Deadman, and tournament worlds.

If the count is wrong, use a manual correction and report the mismatch. Include the crop, patch location, sack type and open/closed state, outfit and diary bonuses, before/after quantities, and the recorded ledger count.
